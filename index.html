Rare Island

Not Just Coconut Oil. A Place of Origin.

Rare Island was born from a simple idea: when you know where something comes from, you understand what makes it special. Our journey begins with coconuts grown on our island farm in Andhra Pradesh, India and ends with a carefully crafted bottle delivered to your home.

Our Origin

Born From Our Island

Rare Island is inspired by our family's connection to coconut farming and the land that has supported generations before us.

Instead of sourcing coconuts from multiple unknown locations, our vision is simple: preserve the identity of the origin.

The result is a single-origin extra-virgin coconut oil created with careful attention from farm to bottle.

Third-generation farm.
One origin. One story. One bottle.
